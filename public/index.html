The house

Room for the
whole crew.

Follow the boardwalk down to the sand, settle into a chair on the shaded deck, or take a dip in the private swim spa. Here, the beach is part of the house’s daily rhythm.

Three levels give everyone a little space. Gather around the long dining table, send the kids down the deck slide, or claim a quiet spot overlooking the water.

Sleeping arrangements

Seven bedrooms include beachfront king rooms and bunk rooms. One king room has a queen-size boat bed for the kids. The house sleeps up to 27 guests.

Kitchen & amenities

Two stoves, two dishwashers, a beverage cooler, and a dining table for 12 make group meals easier. The house also has a game table, laundry facilities, and an elevator.

Parking & pets

Parking accommodates four to six vehicles. Up to two dogs can join you by arrangement with Cape & Coast for an additional fee.
