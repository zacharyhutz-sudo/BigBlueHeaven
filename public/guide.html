Cape San Blas, Florida

Days on the Cape.

Take a morning walk along the dunes, get out on St. Joseph Bay, and bring fresh Gulf seafood back for dinner. Here’s where to go from Big Blue Heaven.

Beaches & outdoors Food & drink Groceries & gear

Beaches &
outdoors

St. Joseph Peninsula State Park

Walk the dunes on the Gulf side or explore trails along the bay. Check the park’s alerts before heading out.

Park details

Salinas Park

Bring a picnic and let the kids spend time on the playground. On the bayside, the Serenity Trail boardwalk looks out over St. Joseph Bay, and there are pickleball courts nearby.

Park details

Loggerhead Run Bike Path

Start near Salinas Park and take the paved trail up the Cape. Turn around wherever you like for an easy out-and-back ride.

Trail details

Cape San Blas Lighthouse

Find the historic lighthouse at George Core Park in Port St. Joe. Pair it with a waterfront walk and a stop downtown.

Lighthouse details

Food & drink

LongBill’s

Seafood, pizza, and tacos right on the Cape. A good stop when everyone wants something different after a beach day.

Menu & hours

Weber’s Little Donut Shop

Pick up cake doughnuts and fried croissants at Scallop Republic on the south end of the Cape.

Shop details

St. Joe Shrimp Company

Bring fresh shrimp, fish, or smoked tuna dip back to the house. Call ahead for a Low Country boil to share.

Market details

Groceries & gear

Scallop Cove

Stop for ice, snacks, and beach supplies. The shop also rents bikes, kayaks, and paddleboards.

Store & rentals

Duren’s Piggly Wiggly

Stock the kitchen for a group at this full grocery store in Port St. Joe.
